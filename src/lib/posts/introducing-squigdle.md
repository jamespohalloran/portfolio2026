A new daily game, and this one's a drawing game!

![Squigdle](https://johalloran.dev/squigdle/img/today-og.png)

Play it Free 🎮: https://johalloran.dev/squigdle/

Every day, everyone gets the same three squiggles. Drag them, spin them, and doodle them into a picture with a limited amount of ink. No undo, so every line counts.

After you create your masterpiece, you can vote on your favorites from the community. The following day you can come back, and see how beloved your artwork was (Remember, art is subjective and it's all for fun!)

Excited to see all the squidles you create!
