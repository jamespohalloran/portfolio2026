New daily game! This one's about mowing your lawn.

![Lawndle](https://johalloran.dev/lawndle/icons/hero.png)

Play it Free 🎮: https://johalloran.dev/lawndle/

Every day there's a new lawn. Cut every blade of grass, mind the pond, and park back in the shed, all in as few steps as you can. It's a small puzzle, but finding the perfect route is oddly satisfying.

This is a little game I've always played in my heard every time I cut the grass. I thought it would make for a fun puzzle game!

Give it a mow, and share your score with a friend.
